# Phase 2 LLM pool and quota ablation (qwen2.5:7b-instruct, dev data, split seed 13)

Generated by `phase2_llm.py` (evaluation) and `phase2_report.py` (`summary.md`, CSVs).
Dev datasets only, 5-fold cross-fit inside TRAIN; TEST never read. One split seed (13), so
the "averaged over split seeds" wording in `summary.md` means one seed here, and the
tuning-stability table is empty (it needs both seed halves). `minus QC` is `nan` because
this design has no unquoted QC arm; the three QC arms differ only in the minority quota.
Descriptive only: counts and medians, no significance claim.

## What the numbers say (F1 at the cross-fitted threshold, median over 7 datasets)

- **Quota (Gate 1 decision 3).** No quota is consistently better than random selection
  from the same admitted pool. For LR: quota 1.0 is +0.007 (5 of 7 datasets above random),
  real share +0.006 (4/7), 0.5 −0.002 (3/7). For RF: quota 1.0 is +0.011 (4/7), 0.5 +0.006
  (5/7), real share −0.022 (1/7). Quota 1.0 is the only arm at or above random for both
  learners. Every difference is within about ±0.02 on 7 datasets with a single split seed.
- **Against REAL_INNER_TRAIN.** For LR, real share is +0.026 (4/7) and random −0.014 (3/7);
  for RF, quota 1.0 is +0.017 (6/7). Mixed, small, and not a basis for a claim.
- **Repair (`llm+round`, vehicle0 only).** All 1,199 candidates were already complete and
  integer-valued, so rounding changed nothing (`complete_unchanged 1199`).

## Pools and copying (copying diagnostics, not privacy results)

- **segment0: no usable pool.** All 140 calls returned (finish reason `stop`), but the
  reply was never the `{"records": [...]}` object the prompt asks for. In fold 0's 28
  replies: 12 were a single bare record, 13 wrapped the records under `examples` (7 with
  an extra `class` key), 2 under `images`, 1 other. The parser rejects these, and the
  pipeline never rewrites a response, so segment0 is reported unavailable (`EMPTY_POOL`),
  not left out.
- **Exact copies of the prompt's reference rows** (equal to the exact copies of inner TRAIN
  in every case): yeast3 196 of 762 candidates (26%), yeast4 47/456, vehicle0 56/1,199,
  Ecoli4 32/573, ecoli1 31/591; Abalone19 and abalone9-18 0. Within-pool exact duplicates
  track the same datasets. All copying comes from the reference rows shown in the prompt,
  as in M1.5.
